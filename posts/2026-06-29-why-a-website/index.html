Why a website?

Work? There's LinkedIn.
Code and projects? GitHub.
Videos? YouTube.
Photos? Instagram.
Trying to shout your ideas at trolls? Facebook.

But there are a few things none of these platforms offer, and that pushed me to build my own space:

  • Ownership. When you open any social media, you're bombarded with content you didn't ask for. I wanted a quiet place where you can read just this if you like it, or leave forever if you don't.
  • Aggregation. I have projects on GitHub, posts scattered on Facebook, a couple of ideas for a YouTube channel, and ideas floating around with no home. I need to put everything in one place.
  • Learning something new. Building a website is a useful skill. It still is in the AI era.

You might ask, why are you not writing in Italian? It would be easier in my native language, but in English I have a larger audience and fewer opportunities to insert terrible puns. The trade-off is worth it.

Update, June 2026: Four years later, I've tried to move the blog from my hand-built GitHub Pages site to Substack. The reasons for writing haven't changed! But maintaining a blog in a static site in my spare time is a losing battle. I thoght that a platform would solve this but also AI can take care of tha maintenance so the blog is staying here.
