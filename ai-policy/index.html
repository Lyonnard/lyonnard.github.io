AI Policy

🧠 The ideas and concepts here are not AI-generated.

Most of what I write starts in moments of boredom or downtime: on a bus, a train, a plane. I jot down notes, then sit down and write when I have time.

The articles are written by me. Sometimes I record myself speaking, and then transcribe the audio to text. I use AI to help with the transcription. I also use AI to fix spelling and translate between Italian and English. Either way, I proofread every translation myself before publishing, and I note on the article when a translation was AI-assisted.

I use AI as a critical reader and sparring partner, to stress-test the validity and soundness of the arguments I make. It gives editing suggestions, but it doesn't rewrite the text. If an image is AI-generated, that's noted under the image.

I make heavy use of AI for the layout and coding (HTML/CSS) of this site.
